01 / Definition

What a GBP-synced website is

A Google Business Profile–synced website is a website that takes supported business information from a Google Business Profile and publishes it on the website automatically. The direction is one way: Google Business Profile → website.

CityWide Digital builds these websites for local businesses and manages the technical side — hosting, the industry template, maintenance and the sync itself. A business owner keeps their Google Business Profile up to date and does not maintain a separate website editor for the information that is synced.

03 / Workflow

How the sync works

  1. Connect the profile

    The business authorises CityWide with their Google account during onboarding. There is no separate content system to learn.

  2. Choose an industry template

    The site is built on a template for the business type — restaurant, contractor, retail and others — configured around the fields the business actually uses.

  3. Change information in Google

    From then on, updates are made in the Google Business Profile: hours, menus, products, posts and replies.

  4. The website follows

    Saved changes are picked up and reflected on the website near real-time. The profile remains the single record of the business information.

What to expect

  • One record to maintain: the Google Business Profile.
  • No website editor, plugin updates or developer on call for synced information.
  • The website stays consistent with what customers see in Google Search and Maps.
  • Google reviews and posts published on the profile appear on the site.
  • Technical hosting and maintenance sit with CityWide Digital, not the business owner.

06 / Exceptions

What the sync does not cover

The sync reflects what exists in the Google Business Profile. Anything that does not exist there is not published by the sync, and needs to be handled separately:

  • Custom page copy, service descriptions and long-form content written for the website
  • Campaign or seasonal landing pages
  • Photography and imagery that is not in the Google profile
  • Bespoke layouts, new sections and structural changes

These are handled as one-off requests through Site Concierge, CityWide's on-demand change service. Pro plans include three Concierge credits per month.

07 / Format

A website, not an app to install

The deliverable is a responsive website served on the customer's domain. There is no installable mobile app and nothing for customers to download. Custom domain support is included on every plan, and the site is built to be readable on phones, tablets and desktops.

10 / Questions

Common questions

Do I still need to update my website separately?

No for anything that is synced from Google. Hours, menus, products, posts, reviews and contact details follow the Google Business Profile. Content that does not exist in Google — custom page copy, campaigns or bespoke layouts — is requested through Site Concierge.

Is the sync one-way or two-way?

One way: Google Business Profile → website. The website does not write information back into Google.

How quickly do changes appear on the website?

Changes appear as soon as they are saved in Google — near real-time. There is no scheduled publishing step to wait for.

What happens if a field does not exist in my Google profile?

It simply is not published by the sync. If the field is missing from Google, the fix is to complete it in the Google Business Profile, or to request that content through Site Concierge if it is not something Google holds.

Do I need technical skills or a developer?

No. CityWide connects the profile, configures the industry template and publishes the site. The business owner approves the design and then manages information in Google.

Can I use my own domain?

Custom domain support is included on every plan, including Starter.

What does it cost?

Starter is $197 per month, Growth $397 and Pro $597, in CAD plus GST. The 30-day trial requires no card and there is no setup fee. You can cancel anytime.

Who owns my business data?

The business retains ownership of its business data, content and materials. CityWide uses that data to provide the service, as described in the Terms of Service.

Is this an app I install?

No. It is a responsive website on a custom domain, not an installable mobile app. The CityWide dashboard is where account and reporting tools live.
