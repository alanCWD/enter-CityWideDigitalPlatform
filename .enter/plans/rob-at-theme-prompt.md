# Rob@ App — CityWide Theme, Branding, Menu & Footer Prompt

## What you get

One paste-ready prompt you can hand to any agent building the **Rob@** app, so the new app looks and reads like the CityWide Digital site it sits beside. It will be delivered in chat **and** saved as `docs/rob-at-app-theme-prompt.md` so you can reuse or edit it later.

It is written as instructions to the builder, not as a description of your site: exact values, exact menu, exact footer, and a clear "do not" list.

## What is already established in the project (and will be quoted in the prompt)

**Brand**: CityWide Digital (one word), positioning line "Managed digital presence for local businesses. Connect your Google Business Profile to everything — automatically.", alan@citywidedigital.ca, 250-882-7378, nationwide, Canadian English. Rob@ is the "Agentic Helpbot" — first contact, books the demo, hands the crew a brief.

**Surfaces** (three, all documented):
- Dark cinematic capsule theme — landing page, near-black canvas.
- Dark glass theme — inner marketing pages: near-black canvas, aurora wash, fine grain, translucent blurred panels, hairline sections.
- Light dashboard/portal theme — electric-blue CTA on cool grey.

The prompt will tell the builder to use the **dark glass surface with the capsule nav/footer** as the Rob@ app's default, and the light token set for signed-in screens. The exact hex/HSL values, type weights, tracking and radii from your current theme will be included so nothing is re-invented.

**Navigation** — fixed, 72px on dark surfaces, wordmark ~35px, five links in IBM Plex Mono 10px uppercase at 0.2em tracking in 55%-opacity ink, active link at full ink, then "Book a demo" plus one bordered trial CTA, profile menu, and a 288px right-hand mobile drawer with a dark backdrop.

**Footer** — #0A1120 (or #05070a on the capsule surface), five-column grid: brand column (wordmark, positioning line, location, email, phone, and the "Ask about CityWide Digital" row of AI assistant icons), Products column with per-product coloured icons, Company column, Guides column, then a bottom bar with copyright and social icons.

## Prompt structure

1. **Role and outcome** — build the Rob@ app as part of the CityWide Digital family, matched pixel-for-pixel in feel, not re-styled.
2. **Brand lockup and facts** — names, spelling, logo file and its dark-surface treatment, contact details, attribution line.
3. **Colour and materials** — the exact tokens, the near-black canvas, the aurora wash, the grain, the glass recipe, and the single blue accent rule.
4. **Typography** — Inter light display type, IBM Plex Mono kickers, the four type roles and their weights, tracking and line-heights.
5. **The shell** — the fixed nav and the footer, described by behaviour at desktop and mobile, including the shrink-on-scroll rule, active states, hover states and the drawer.
6. **Menu items** — Services, Pricing, About, Crew, Contact, plus Book a demo and the trial CTA, with the rule that only live pages are linked.
7. **Components** — glass panels (default, soft, feature, hover), app-coloured panels, primary and ghost buttons, chips, form fields, tables, accordions.
8. **Page recipes** — hero, section rhythm, hairline dividers, closing CTA band, so new pages land on the same grid (1240px shell, 4.5–6.5rem section padding).
9. **Guardrails** — no new brand colours, no custom styles outside the tokens, lucide icons only, no emoji, no invented routes, reduced-motion respected, accessible focus states, Canadian English.

## Outcomes

- [ ] The prompt states the brand facts, logo treatment, contact details and attribution rule exactly as the live site does.
- [ ] It gives the builder the complete colour, typography and material values, so no new colour, font or glass recipe is introduced.
- [ ] It describes the fixed nav and footer by behaviour at both desktop and mobile widths, including scroll, active, hover and drawer states.
- [ ] It lists the exact menu items and the rule that only live pages are linked from nav and footer.
- [ ] It covers the reusable components and page rhythm a new page needs to sit on the same grid.
- [ ] It ends with the guardrails that keep the app from drifting into its own visual identity.
- [ ] The prompt is saved to `docs/rob-at-app-theme-prompt.md` and shown in chat, ready to paste.

## Boundaries

- No changes to the live CityWide pages, nav, footer or theme files — this is a written handoff, not a redesign.
- The prompt targets the app that will be built elsewhere; if you would rather I build the Rob@ app screens inside this project instead, say so and I will plan that separately.
